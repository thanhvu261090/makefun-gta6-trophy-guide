    About this guide

    • The list: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned. All single-player; there are no online trophies.
    • Time: around 200 hours to the platinum, most of it the 100% checklist. One playthrough is enough.
    • Missable: only Positive Mental Attitude can be missed — Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. Everything else stays available after the story. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.
    • Cheats: using a cheat disables trophies and saving on that save, so keep cheats off your trophy run.
    • Sources: trophy names, descriptions and grades as published on PlayStation, listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (reported by Push Square, 18 November 2026). Each guide is written in our own words and cites its sources — see the source links beside every trophy. Guides used: GTA Base, Output Lag, KeenGamer.
    • Unconfirmed steps: where a trophy’s exact steps were still unconfirmed at launch, the guide says so instead of guessing.
    • Privacy: this guide runs entirely offline — it fetches nothing at run time — and your checklist never leaves your browser unless you export it yourself.
